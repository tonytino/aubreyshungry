- `[propagate]` `/generate-week` now asks a second question in the same AskUserQuestion exchange as the target week: what fresh ingredients are already on hand and will spoil during the plan week (issue #27). The plan is drafted to eat them, ordered by perishability — the most perishable land Sunday–Tuesday, since a Sunday→Saturday week outlives a punnet of berries — and they land naturally in snacks, sweet and yogurt-style dishes, and finishing touches. Skipping is a valid answer and never blocks generation; the golden rules (`docs/agents/dietary-safety.md`) still override on-hand input, and anything excluded for a Rule 1/2 violation must be named in the PR body rather than silently dropped. Quantities are never invented for on-hand items, and because the shopping list is derived from recipes those items still appear on it — the week's `notes` and the PR body say which lines to cross off. Mirrored in `.claude/skills/generate-week/SKILL.md` and `docs/agents/generation.md`.
- `[manual]` Generation no longer asks — or hedges in prose — about whether a food group is acceptable (issue #27). The standing rule is now recorded in `.claude/skills/generate-week/SKILL.md` and `docs/agents/generation.md`: assume every food group is fine unless the golden rules forbid it or it appears in `avoidIngredients` in `content/preferences.json`. The dairy conditional on plain yogurt is removed from `docs/agents/nutrition-guidelines.md`; product-level gluten-free label checks (tempeh, kimchi/kombucha, spice blends, oats, broths) are untouched and still mandatory. Manual because the behavior now leans entirely on `content/preferences.json`: before taking this, confirm your instance's `avoidIngredients` actually lists the household's soft dislikes, since the generator will stop asking about them. `avoidIngredients` is for dislikes only and is never a safety mechanism (`app/content/preferences.ts` is authoritative) — a genuine intolerance does not belong there and is not covered by it, so record those with the owner directly.
